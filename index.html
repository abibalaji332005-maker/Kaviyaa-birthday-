For Kaviya

Dear Kaviya,

Happy Birthday.

I know we’ve been fighting for a long time and we don’t really talk much anymore. I didn’t make this to pressure you or ask for anything—I just know you haven’t been okay lately, and I wanted to leave a quiet little piece of light for you today, hoping it makes things feel even slightly better.

No matter how messy things got between us, you are still one of the very first friends I made in college—the first girl I actually wanted to be friends with. When I think back, it’s always the stupid, unforgettable stuff that hits first: holding your melting ice cream because you’re genuinely the worst ice cream eater alive, fighting like we’re still in first year, calling you Kaviya the Nun, and watching a 0-viewer vlogger still act like she’s filming a documentary.

Beyond all the jokes, I’ll never forget how you took care of me like my own mother did. You have this habit of thinking that sacrificing your own happiness is what friendship is supposed to look like—and I really hope this year is the year you stop doing that. You deserve the exact same kindness you give everyone else.

You are the strongest woman I have ever seen. I know you’ve lost your way a little right now, and it’s completely okay to feel tired. Just don’t forget the unstoppable badass you were—she’s still in there, and I know you’ll find your way back to her.

More than anything, Kaviya, I hope this year brings you peace and brings back your loudest, happiest laugh. Take care of yourself.

— Niranjan
